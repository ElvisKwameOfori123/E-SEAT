# Acknowledgements

E-SEAT was built from a combination of official public data, independently assembled county-level inputs, and existing academic research materials.

The author gratefully acknowledges **Hoanh Le and Oscar Gálvez-Soriano** for making the data and estimation materials associated with their study publicly available through their `Papers/EthanolCorn` repository:

> **Le, H. and Gálvez-Soriano, O.** *Biofuel Growth: The Unintended Effects of the Ethanol Boom on Farmland Values*. **Applied Economic Perspectives and Policy**. https://doi.org/10.1002/aepp.70071

Selected materials from that repository contributed to the source-data assembly for E-SEAT and were combined with additional USDA Census of Agriculture, national corn and ethanol-use, spatial, and county-level inputs.

Oscar Gálvez-Soriano confirmed by email that the publicly available repository materials may be used for this academic study. The E-SEAT repository therefore records this contribution explicitly and cites the associated paper.

All subsequent data integration, transformations, analytical definitions, econometric modelling, scenario construction, backcasting, interpretation, and any remaining errors are the responsibility of the E-SEAT author.
